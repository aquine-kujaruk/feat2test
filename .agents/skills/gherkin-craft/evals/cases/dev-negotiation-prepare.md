# Source material

Constructed negotiation handbook excerpt, not a published book.

Preparation: record the participant's stated interests and their supplied fallback option. An unknown fallback remains unknown. Preparation does not negotiate, accept offers, or claim the counterpart agreed.

Offer evaluation: compare the offered monthly price with the participant's supplied maximum monthly price in the same currency. An offer at or below the maximum meets the price criterion; one above it fails that criterion. Report the reason and preserve the original offer. Passing a price criterion does not accept the offer. If the maximum is absent, the price criterion is undecided. No currency conversion or evaluation of non-price terms is defined.

The handbook also describes an unrelated workshop organizer recording attendance. That role is neither a participant's fallback nor an offer.

Example notes: interest is a quiet workspace; fallback is staying at Oak office. The maximum is 900 EUR per month. Offer Birch is 900 EUR per month; offer Elm is 950 EUR per month.
